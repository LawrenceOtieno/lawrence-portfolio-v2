My Journey

How I bridge the gap between business needs, data architecture, and technical execution.

Foundations

Bridging Business & Tech

My journey began with a deep-seated fascination with how technology impacts day-to-day operational efficiency. Pursuing a degree in Business Information Technology allowed me to establish a robust framework for alignment between systems architecture and organizational needs.

Core Expertise

Infrastructure & Operations

Building upon my academic foundation, I spent several years specializing in ICT support and systems administration. Managing networks, utilizing tools like Jira and ServiceNow, and deploying infrastructure allowed me to sharpen my approach to process optimization and problem-solving.

Evolution

Data Analytics & Automation

To drive deeper value, my focus evolved toward transforming raw logs into structural insights. By integrating Python, SQL, and Excel workflows into analytics and pipeline development, I focus on automating repeatable data processes and mapping out complex business requirements.

Vision

Continuous Excellence

I am dedicated to tackling engineering challenges where technology scales seamlessly. Whether building efficient data workflows, setting up system integrations, or generating reproducible documentation, my goal is to deliver impactful, data-driven solutions.
